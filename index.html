Android Automation Utility

AutoTap

AutoTap is an Android automation utility that allows users to configure numbered tap points and execute tap sequences using selectable timing profiles. Settings and automation points are controlled by the user on their own device.

Features

Configurable Tap Points

Add and arrange numbered automation points for supported workflows directly on your Android device.

Timing Profiles

Choose an available total sequence timing profile according to your active access plan.

Device-Based Settings

Automation configuration and supported application credentials remain on the user's device.

Policies

Privacy

AutoTap uses information required to provide account, device and paid access functionality. Supported application credentials and local automation settings are intended to remain on the user's device. Payment processing is handled through the configured payment provider.

Payments and Access

Paid plans provide access for the period displayed at purchase. A plan is activated only after successful payment verification. Failed, cancelled or pending payments do not provide paid access.

Cancellation and Refunds

Users can choose not to renew after their current weekly access period. Since plans use manual renewal, cancelling future access does not require cancelling an automatic subscription. Eligible refunds, if applicable, are handled according to the payment and support process.
